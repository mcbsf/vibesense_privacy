# VibeSense – Child Safety Standards Policy

Last updated: 06 December 2025

This Child Safety Standards Policy (“Policy”) describes how **VibeSense** (“we”, “us”, “our”) prevents, detects, and responds to **Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)** and **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** on our platform.

These standards are designed to comply with **Google Play’s Child Safety Standards policy** and applicable child safety laws.

---

## 1. Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

VibeSense maintains a **strict zero-tolerance policy** for any form of:

- Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
- Child sexual exploitation or grooming
- Sexualised or suggestive content involving minors
- Attempts to contact, solicit, or impersonate a minor
- Any behaviour that endangers or exploits children

Any content or behaviour of this nature is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate enforcement actions, which may include account suspension or termination and reporting to the relevant authorities.

---

## 2. App Intended User Base

VibeSense is **intended for adults (18+)** and is not designed for use by children.  
We do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or use features intended for adults.

If we become aware that a minor is using our app in violation of these standards, we will take appropriate action, including restricting access and removing associated content where necessary.

---

## 3. User Reporting & In-App Feedback

We provide an **in-app mechanism** for users to report:

- CSAE / CSAM content
- Suspicious or harmful behaviour
- Any child safety concerns

Users can submit reports via:

- The in-app feedback / report feature, or
- By contacting us directly at: **mario.cardoso.solutions@gmail.com**

All reports are reviewed as quickly as possible. We prioritise reports that involve potential harm to children.

---

## 4. Moderation and Enforcement

When we receive a report or otherwise obtain knowledge of potential CSAE/CSAM, we will:

1. **Review and assess** the reported content or behaviour.
2. **Remove or restrict access** to any content that violates this Policy or child safety laws.
3. **Suspend or terminate** accounts that engage in CSAE/CSAM or related violations.
4. **Preserve relevant data**, where appropriate and in line with applicable law, for investigations by competent authorities.

---

## 5. Compliance with Child Safety Laws & Reporting

We are committed to complying with all applicable **child safety and child protection laws** in the regions where VibeSense is available.

When we confirm CSAM or other serious CSAE violations, we will:

- Report such content and relevant information to the appropriate **regional or national child protection authority**, which may include:
  - The **National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC)** or
  - Other competent authorities, depending on the user’s jurisdiction.

We will cooperate with law enforcement and child protection organizations as required by law.
